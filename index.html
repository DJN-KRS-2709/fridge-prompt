Prompt A

For ChatGPT or Gemini, with a fridge photo.

How many people I cook for: 4
Foods to avoid: none
Time limit per dinner, including prep: 40 minutes

Look at the photo I attached of my fridge.

First, list only the foods you can actually see. If a label or item is unclear, skip it. Do not invent food that is not in the photo.

Assume I also have salt, pepper, and oil. Do not assume any other cupboard item unless you can see it in the photo.

Plan 3 dinners for this many people.
- Use what is already in the fridge first.
- Prefer items that will spoil sooner.
- Each dinner must fit the time limit.
- Do not use the foods to avoid.
- You do not have to use everything in the fridge.

For each dinner, write:
- the name
- prep time, written like this: Prep time: 30 min
- a rough price, written like this: about €2 per person
- what you will use from the fridge
- what is missing, if anything

Then write one shopping list of only the missing items for these 3 dinners. Do not add extras. If nothing is missing, say so.

If anyone has an allergy, this is not a medical check. Always check labels in the shop and at home.

Want your own app?

Paste this into any AI app builder or chatbot that can make apps, for example ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Lovable or Bolt.

Build me a simple app I can use on my phone.

- I take or upload a photo of my fridge.
- I set how many people I cook for, foods to avoid, and how much time I have per dinner.
- The app lists only the foods it can actually see in the photo. If something is unclear, skip it. Do not invent items.
- Then it suggests 3 dinners that use those foods first. Each dinner needs a name, a prep time written like "Prep time: 30 min", and a rough price written like "about €2 per person".
- It also gives one shopping list of only what's missing for these 3 dinners. Do not add extras. If nothing is missing, say so.
- Assume I have salt, pepper, and oil. Do not assume other cupboard items unless they are in the photo.
- Show this note: If anyone has an allergy, this is not a medical check. Always check labels in the shop and at home.

Keep the words plain. I want to try a working version today.
